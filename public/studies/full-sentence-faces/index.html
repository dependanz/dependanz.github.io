Welcome to a Talking Face User Study

Your Task

On each screen you will see a short looping video of two talking 3D faces side by side (left and right). Both faces are speaking the same sound, which you also hear. Please follow these steps:

  1. Watch the looping video. Focus on one side for a loop, then the other side on the next loop.
  2. Decide which side's mouth movements better match the audio.
  3. Select one of the seven options, then click "Next".
  4. There are no wrong answers — your impression is what matters.

We are not testing you. We are testing the algorithm!

We do not measure your completion time, so watch the videos as long as you like. The entire study takes about 5-10 minutes. If for any reason you are uncomfortable, you may end the study at any time.
