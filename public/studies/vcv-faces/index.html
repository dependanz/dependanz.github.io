Talking Face Animation Study

Your Task

On each screen you will see a short looping video of two talking 3D faces side by side (left and right). Both faces speak the same audio, which you also hear.

  1. Watch the looping video. Focus on one side for a loop, then the other on the next loop.
  2. Decide which side's mouth movements better match the audio.
  3. Select one of the seven options, then click "Next".
  4. There are no wrong answers — your impression is what matters.

The next two screens are a short consent form and a quick sound check (please have your headphones ready) — then the study begins.

Information & Consent

You are invited to take part in a research study conducted by researchers at the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), on how people perceive computer-generated talking-face animations.

If you agree, you will watch several short looping videos of two animated 3D faces side by side, each speaking the same audio, and for each you will rate which side's mouth movements better match what you hear. The study takes about 8–10 minutes.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time by closing the window. Risks are minimal — no more than ordinary computer use (possible mild eye strain or fatigue). There is no direct benefit to you other than the compensation shown on the recruitment page.

Privacy. We do not collect your name, email, or any identifying information, and the study does not use your camera or microphone. We record only your ratings, response times, and the anonymous participant code from the recruitment platform (used only to pay you). Results are stored securely and reported only in aggregate.

Eligibility. You must be at least 18 years old and a fluent English speaker.

Questions about the study: ds867@njit.edu. For questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the NJIT Institutional Review Board (IRB) at irb@njit.edu.
